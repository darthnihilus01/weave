Morning Meditative Guide

Quiet Morning Coffee Ritual Companion

Slow your pulse. Calibrate your kettle to 93.0°C. Follow the pulse-pour rhythm designed to preserve the delicate jasmine florals without over-extracting bitter tannins.

Ritual Stopwatch Phase: Bloom
00:00
01 • 0:00 - 0:45 Weight: 45g H2O

The Degas & Gentle Bloom

Pour 45g of water in gentle concentric spirals from center outwards. Swirl brewer once with two fingertips. Allow trapped roasting CO₂ to escape and liberate the jasmine aromatics.

02 • 0:45 - 1:20 Weight: 100g H2O (Total: 145g)

Laminar Core Pour

Pour at a calm 3.0 mL/s directly into the core bed. Keep kettle spout low (5cm above bed) to minimize air turbulence and cavitation.

03 • 1:20 - 1:55 Weight: 60g H2O (Total: 205g)

Peripheral Rinse Pulse

Gently wash down fine coffee particles clinging to paper walls. Promote flat, uniform bed subsidence.

04 • 1:55 - 2:45 Weight: 45g H2O (Total: 250g)

Final Drawdown & Stillness

Center pour to achieve 250g total weight. Allow full gravity percolation drawdown. Total target time: 2:45. Let cup cool to 58°C before first sip.
